03Method

How a coin trends.

The order matters. A narrative shows up in launch names first, in callers’ posts and smart wallets second, and on your timeline last. Most new tokens go to zero: the print shows where attention is heading, not what to buy.

  1. 1

    Catch the word

    Moth shows words being launched faster than usual. A word marked New or ×3 that keeps climbing is a narrative forming.

    Moth Radar
  2. 2

    Find callers who deliver

    Kolibri shows what every call did a minute, an hour and a day after the post. Skip the loud ones. Follow the A’s.

    Kolibri Search
  3. 3

    Pass the gate

    Before anything else: who holds the supply, who bought in the first block, can the mint still print. What fails never makes the feed.

    Chameleonscope
  4. 4

    Follow proven wallets

    On Robinhood Chain, several traders with a record entering one token within minutes says more than any post.

    Fomo Radar
  5. 5

    Enter on rules

    A score with its reasons written next to it, an entry only once the opening tax has decayed, the price of leaving known before you need it.

    Loxley
  6. 6

    Watch the door

    After the buy, the question is the exit: the deployer’s share, the reserve, the pool. LEAVE is a measured change, not a feeling.

    Canary

Kolibri SearchReport cards for callers

Who called it.

Paste an X account. Kolibri finds every token it called and measures each call: the market cap when the post went out, and what it was 1 minute, 5 minutes, 1 hour and 24 hours later.

Whole X accounts open once the server has an X data key. A link to a single post works now.

Leaderboard

    How grades work
    A call
    The first time an account posts a token’s $TICKER or contract address. Posts of the same token within 7 days count as one call. Posts listing more than six tokens are commentary. Reposts of other people’s posts don’t count.
    Call mcap
    The market cap in the minute the post went out, from 1-minute candles.
    1m, 5m, 1h, 24h
    The market cap that long after the post, against the call mcap: called at $20K and $40K a minute later is 2.0x; $10K is −50%. Launchpad tokens are followed into the pool they migrated to.
    Peak 24h
    The best 1-minute close in the first hour and 15-minute close after it, within 24 hours.
    Grade
    Score 0–100 from the 1-hour results: 60% the share of calls up after an hour, 40% the median 1-hour result, pulled toward 50 while there are few calls. A ≥ 75, B ≥ 62, C ≥ 50, D ≥ 38, F below. Shown once 3 calls have a 1-hour result.

            How to read Moth

            1. Switch to Rising and 15m. Top shows words that are always big, like ai, cat and dog. Rising shows words launched faster than usual right now.
            2. Look for a word that jumps. It says New or ×3, its bars grow toward the right, and it keeps climbing over a few refreshes.
            3. Read the line under the word. That is the token with this word people are trading right now, its market cap and its move over 5 minutes. A bright dot means it traded in the last 90 seconds, a ring means it graduated to PumpSwap. Dumped tokens never show there.
            4. Open the word. “Trading now” lists the best tokens behind it. “Launched together with” tells you what the story is.
            5. Press Search on X. If nobody is posting about it yet, you are early. If everyone is, you are late.
            6. Star the words you care about. Moth tells you here when a watched word heats up.

            Moth shows what people are launching, not what to buy. Most new tokens go to zero and many are rugs, so check the token’s page, holders and developer before you trade.
